## Name

Slot Panic

## Short description

One-finger slot car racing: lift before the bend or you fly off

## Full description

Slot Panic is top-down slot car racing with a single control: the trigger. Your car is locked into its lane; there is no steering. Hold your finger down to accelerate and lift it to slow down. Take a bend too fast and the tail slides out; keep pushing and the car flies off, spins across the table and a hand puts it back on the track.

HOW TO PLAY
• Hold to accelerate and release to slow down. Tap it and the power stays halfway, just like a real controller's trigger.
• Past the grip limit the car slides and loses speed; it only flies off if you keep pushing.
• Flying off costs time, not the race: the hand puts the car back where it left the track.
• Five laps. The cars swap lanes every race.

AGAINST THE CPU OR TWO PLAYERS ON ONE SCREEN
No menus: the left half of the screen is the red car and the right half is the blue one. Each player takes a lane by pressing; any lane nobody touches is driven by a CPU that makes mistakes too.

THREE TRACKS
• The Hairpin: two fast bends and a finger with a tight hairpin.
• The Figure Eight: a crossing where the cars can crash, a banked hairpin, a chicane and two drift bends.
• The Knot: a crossover where the lanes cross and the cars swap lanes, a bumpy straight, a hairpin and a chicane.

Four settings —grass, dirt, desert and snow— by day, at sunset or at night under the floodlights.

OFFLINE, NO ACCOUNT, NO ADS
• Works fully offline.
• No sign-up, no ads, no purchases and no data collected.
• In English, Spanish, Portuguese, Galician, Catalan, Italian and French.

You can also play it in your browser at slot.pnyk.es.
