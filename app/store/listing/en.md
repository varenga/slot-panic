## Name

Slot Panic

## Short description

One-finger slot car racing: lift before the bend or you fly off

## Full description

Slot Panic is top-down slot car racing with a single control: the trigger. Your car is locked into its lane; there is no steering. Hold your finger down to accelerate and lift it to slow down. Take a bend too fast and the tail slides out; keep pushing and the car flies off, spins across the table and a hand puts it back on the track.

HOW TO PLAY
• Hold to accelerate, release to slow down. Tap it and the power stays halfway, like the trigger of a real controller.
• Past the grip limit the car slides and loses speed; it only flies off if you keep pushing.
• Flying off costs time, not the race: the hand puts the car back where it left the track.
• Three, five or ten laps. The cars swap lanes every race, and at crossovers, crossings and narrow sections, if they arrive together, they crash.

AGAINST THE CPU, TWO PLAYERS OR ONLINE
• Against a CPU that makes mistakes too, at three levels.
• Two players on the same screen: the left half is the red car and the right half, the blue one.
• Online: create a room and give the four-letter code to whoever is racing you, each on their own phone. No accounts: without the code nobody gets in. If someone leaves, you carry on against the CPU.
• With the phone upright, magnifier mode: the track zoomed in around your car and the map on top.

CIRCUITS
Six two-lane circuits built from toy pieces: crossovers where the cars swap lanes, crossings, banked bends, chicanes, bumps, narrow sections where the lanes come together, a skid bend and a bridge that slows you down going up and pushes you coming down. And the four-lane track, with three more circuits and four cars; the ones that are not a figure of eight are raced in heats, one per lane, and the lowest total wins.

MORE
• Championship: the six circuits in a row.
• Thirteen cars, each with its own character: acceleration, grip and top speed.
• Online lap records on the official circuits, with the record holder’s ghost.
• A replay of the best moments at the end of every race.
• Draw your own circuit with your finger on a grid, try it and publish it in the gallery for anyone to race.
• Five settings —one of them a wooden table with a plastic track— by day, at sunset or at night.

NO ACCOUNT, NO ADS
• Plays offline; the network is only needed for the gallery, the records and online races.
• No sign-up, no ads and no purchases.
• In Spanish, English, Portuguese, Galician, Catalan, Italian and French.

You can also play it in the browser at slot.pnyk.es.
